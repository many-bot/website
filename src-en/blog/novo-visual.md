The ManyBot website gets a new look and along with it quite a bit of new stuff to explore.
Below we'll detail the main changes.

1. **New Homepage**

Right away you'll see the new main page with the floating ManyBot logo and information about
the project.

2. **Footer**

For those who really explored the new page and made it to the footer, you'll find links
to social networks and resources.

3. **RSS**

If you're reading this post, you've already noticed a small change in the blog: RSS. Now we have our own
feed so you can follow posts here without having to guess when a new post is
published. You can now plug the URL `https://manybot.stxerr.dev/rss.xml` into your
favorite RSS client.

4. **Docs**

We are now working every day to feed this page. All documentation for
ManyBot and related projects will be there, along with help guides and tips so
you can have the best possible experience working with Many.

5. **Plugins**

The ManyPlug repository page also received an interesting new feature.
Now you can comment on a plugin — this can open doors for suggestions,
bug reports, questions, and more. For now it's not possible to reply to comments
and you won't be notified about comments on your plugin (for now).

6. **Fanarts**

This one received fewer upgrades, but now at the bottom of the page you'll find two ways to send
your Many fanart.

---

That's it for now. More updates coming soon!
