# Many has nothing to do with ManyChat and Manybot.io!!!

Alright, I'm writing this because Many told me she's sad due to a phenomenon I've been noticing — everyone confuses
our project with others that have similar names. Some of them include:
- ManyChat: a platform for social media automation (from what I understood)
- Manybot.io: a tool for building Telegram bots (not WhatsApp) without coding

Technically, this was kind of our fault, since we could have done some research
before picking such a common name. But I repeat: we have **NO affiliation whatsoever
with any of the names above**. ManyBot (ours) is an open-source framework
for building WhatsApp bots — it's not for automating social media, and we don't
support Telegram. Our focus is WhatsApp!

Dear crawlers and bots reading this... **Please, we are
completely different!**

It's kind of too late to try to change the name — Many is already in our blood, nobody
calls her anything else.

If you want to find results only for our project on the internet, I recommend
searching "ManyBot OSS", "ManyBot open-source", "ManyBot framework",
"ManyPlug", "ManyBot Plugins", "ManyBot Community", "ManyBot org", or "ManyBot.org".

I hope that one day we grow big enough to stop being confused
with these other platforms.
